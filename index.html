About MIX DIG PAY Architecture

MIX DIG PAY is a high-performance utility platform built to streamline collections for independent creators, merchants, and enterprises. It generates instant, permanent, and non-expiring fixed-amount UPI QR codes with zero processing overhead.

  • Direct P2P Settlement: Funds route directly into your configured UPI ID and bank account. We never act as a payment gateway intermediary.
  • Multi-Merchant Memory: Securely manage multiple business profiles locally in your browser storage with zero repetitive data entry.
  • Universal Compatibility: Fully optimized for Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, and all banking UPI applications.

Important Notice & Disclaimer:

Payment directly aapke apne UPI ID / bank account me jayegi. Kripya payment hone ke baad apna banking app khud check karein. Ye website kisi bhi prakar ka payment processing ya gateway handle nahi karti, ye sirf aapke diye gaye UPI ID aur amount ka dynamic QR generate karti hai. Kisi bhi payment failure, transaction dispute ya payment loss ki saari zimmedari keval aapki (merchant ki) hogi, is website ya developer ki koi zimmedari nahi hai.
